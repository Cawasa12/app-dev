## My Favorite Series: All of us are dead
*I think All of Us Are Dead is my favorite because it's not just your typical zombie series. It has action, suspense, friendship, romance, and emotional moments that actually make you care about the characters.*

*I like how the story shows different personalities and how people act when they're put in a situation where survival is everything. You get attached to the characters, and every time someone dies, it actually hurts. The series also keeps you interested because you never really know what's going to happen next.*

# Story Summary
A group of high school students becomes trapped inside their school after a zombie outbreak suddenly spreads. With limited supplies and no way to contact the outside world, they must find a way to survive while facing difficult decisions.

## Genre
| Genre | **Description** |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| Horror | Focuses on zombies, frightening situations, and terrifying moments that keep viewers on edge. |
| Thriller | Creates suspense and excitement as the students struggle to survive and escape the zombie outbreak. |
